FAQ

Questions diners and restaurants actually ask.

Appertivo is built around same-day restaurant specials, so the answers below focus on freshness, accuracy, approval, and how specials get published.

For diners

How current are the specials?

Specials are designed to be same-day posts. Cards show a date, and restaurants can mark a special sold out before its scheduled expiration.

Are these current restaurant specials?

Appertivo is built for timely restaurant posts. Cards show a date, and restaurants can update or remove a special when details change.

Do I need an account to browse?

No. You can browse specials, view restaurants, get directions, call, and share links without creating an account.

Can I order directly from Appertivo?

Not yet. When a restaurant provides an ordering link, Appertivo can point you to their own ordering platform instead of taking the order here.

Why do some specials disappear?

Specials are intentionally temporary. They may expire at the end of the day, after a weekend window, or when a restaurant marks them sold out.

How do I follow one restaurant?

Use the alert form on the restaurant page. That lets us notify you when that restaurant has fresh specials available.

What areas does Appertivo cover?

Appertivo currently focuses on Skagit Valley restaurants. Diners in other areas can sign up for notifications as coverage expands.

What if a price or detail is wrong?

The restaurant is the source of truth. Check the live restaurant link or call before making a trip for a limited special.

Can I share a special with someone?

Yes. Each live special has its own public link that can be shared by text, email, or social apps.

Why focus on specials instead of full menus?

Full menus are often static. Specials are timely, local, and decision-making friendly when you are choosing where to eat tonight.

For restaurants

How do I publish my first special?

Use Get Started, search for your restaurant, add your email, paste the special details, and upload a photo if you have one.

Do I need to create a full account first?

No. Your first special starts the workflow. You confirm the restaurant when you approve and publish the draft from your email.

Can I just reply to an outreach email?

Yes. If you reply with the special and image, Appertivo can turn that into an enhanced draft and send it back for approval.

What does Appertivo change before publishing?

We clean up spelling and grammar, tighten the copy, prepare the image when possible, and keep the offer faithful to what you sent.

Do I approve the draft before it goes live?

Yes. You receive an approval email with a Publish button. Nothing has to go live until you approve it.

How fast does the draft come back?

Most drafts should be ready within a few minutes when the automated enhancement flow is available.

Can I mark a special sold out early?

Yes. When you publish from your email, that browser gets access to operator tools on the live special, including Sold Out.

Can I add an Order Now button?

Order links are planned as an operator tool. They will point directly to your own ordering platform, not a separate Appertivo cart.

What does it cost?

Restaurants can submit a special through the current pilot workflow. Paid operator tools are separate from the basic submission flow.

What if my restaurant is not listed yet?

Search by Google Places on Get Started. If your restaurant is in the service area, you can submit details and be added to the directory.
